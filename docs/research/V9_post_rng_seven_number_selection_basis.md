# V9 primary-source basis: post-RNG seven-number selection propensity

Date: 2026-08-16

Research status: **deferred / prospective-only if ever revived**. This is a
primary-source review and mathematical derivation only; no V9 historical score,
backtest, prediction, or outcome report was run or inspected. On 2026-08-16 the
project chose to freeze the unchanged V3 algorithm for a clean prospective
cohort instead of implementing V9.

Proposed experiment slug: `V9_post_rng_seven_number_selection_propensity`

## Bottom line

The source review supports only one marginally defensible V9 hypothesis from
the fields that this repository actually has:

> **H9:** In the current RNG regime, a stable number-specific propensity to
> enter the seven-number set consisting of six main numbers plus the bonus may
> persist prospectively. A strictly prior estimate of that total selection
> propensity may therefore improve future six-main-number probabilities over
> constant `6/49`.

This is a narrow *selection-stage* probe. It is not a claim that the lottery is
predictable and not a new form of “hot number” folklore. Under the official fair
draw model, every label has seven-set probability `7/49 = 1/7`, past labels
contain no information about future labels, and the proposed model should fail.

**Recommendation:** H9 is deferred and is at most a prospective-only
pre-registration candidate after a separate future review. Do not run another
V9 historical performance search. All observed
draws may initialize one frozen expanding estimator, but no observed result may
select its feature, smoothing, transform, control, metric, or activation
decision. If an independent review considers the overlap with ordinary
main-number frequency too large, the correct decision is to stop rather than
invent a residue, bit-width, physical-ball proxy, or window. If it is accepted,
the first evidentiary outcome must be an immutable pre-draw snapshot for a draw
after the freeze commit.

## Facts established by primary sources

1. The official ILC conditions define a Classic Draw as six main numbers and
   one bonus number, all seven different, drawn at random from `1..49`:
   [ILC LOTTO 6/49 Game Conditions, section
   4](https://corporate.bclc.com/content/dam/bclccorporate/documents/terms-and-conditions/rules-and-regulations/lotto/lotto-649-game-conditions.pdf).
   Thus the seven-number union is an official observable, not a set reconstructed
   from unrelated fields.

2. The applicable draw mechanism changed on 2019-05-15. Atlantic Lottery's
   official notice says that LOTTO 6/49 winning numbers on that date would be
   generated by RNG software, and its integrity page says RNG replaced the
   mechanical ball-drop machines: [transition
   notice](https://www.alc.ca/content/dam/alc/docs-en/Corp/AboutAL/Retailers/LMx2_Sellsheet_EN.PDF)
   and [integrity
   statement](https://www.alc.ca/content/alc/en/corporate/about-atlantic-lottery/integrity-and-compliance.html).
   A current-software hypothesis must not pool the old mechanical regime into
   its estimator.

3. WCLC says current national and regional draws use RNG software on
   stand-alone computerized draw machines. It says the proprietary algorithm is
   independently tested and certified, an integrity check occurs before every
   draw, and external auditors witness all draws: [WCLC Game & Draw
   Security](https://www.wclc.com/game-draw-security.htm?channel=print).
   OLG says GLI reviewed and tested the actual ILC RNG software used for LOTTO
   6/49 and certified random behaviour, with external auditors operating from
   two locations: [OLG LOTTO 6/49 RNG
   FAQ](https://www.olg.ca/en/frequently-asked-questions/lottery-games/lotto-649.html).
   These facts give the fair, unpredictable null very high prior weight.

4. GLI's public testing instructions distinguish final game outcomes from raw
   RNG output before scaling or shuffling. They ask for production-equivalent
   functions, calls, variables, scaling methodology, draw range, and
   without-replacement rules, plus a separate raw-output tool when required:
   [GLI Technical Specifications for RNG
   Testing](https://gaminglabs.com/getting-started/technical-specifications-for-rng-testing/).
   Public winning sets are therefore much weaker evidence than the materials
   available to a certifying laboratory. They can reveal a persistent final
   label association but cannot locate an entropy, scaling, shuffling, or
   publication fault.

5. GLI-11 is a general public standard, not proof of the private ILC engagement
   specification. It nevertheless describes the relevant null: source review
   covers core RNG, scaling, mapping, and shuffling functions; final output is
   tested for its intended distribution and independence; each possible RNG
   selection is required to be equally likely. See [GLI-11 v3.0, sections
   3.2.1--3.2.4](https://gaminglabs.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/GLI-11-Gaming-Devices-V3-0.pdf).

6. Sets drawn without replacement have dependent within-draw label indicators.
   Lottery-specific original research develops uniformity tests and unequal
   selection-probability inference for this setting: [Joe
   (1993)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-7152(93)90141-5) and [Johnson & Klotz
   (1993)](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1993.10476320). A valid V9 control or
   uncertainty calculation must therefore preserve complete seven-label rows.

## Mechanism boundary: selection versus role

For label `i` and draw `d`, let

```text
M_d = the set of six main numbers
B_d = the one bonus number
U_d = M_d union {B_d}.
```

The main-number inclusion probability factors as

```text
P(i in M_d)
  = P(i in U_d) * P(i in M_d | i in U_d).
```

Under fair exchangeability,

```text
P(i in U_d)            = 7/49 = 1/7
P(i in M_d | i in U_d) = 6/7
P(i in M_d)            = 6/49.
```

V7 tested the second factor: conditional main-versus-bonus role odds after a
label was already among the seven. H9 collapses the role and tests only the
first factor: whether a label enters the seven-number set at all. No V9 feature
may use a historical main/bonus contrast, and the target bonus remains excluded
from prediction and scoring.

One possible interpretation of a stable first-factor departure is a final
selection, mapping, or publication association. That interpretation is only a
motivation. The proprietary algorithm, raw outputs, audit records, and
production metadata are unavailable, so outcome history cannot identify a
cause or establish a software defect.

## The one fixed candidate formula

This is a source-bounded formula that a later experiment registration could
freeze verbatim. It is not permission to score history before registration and
review.

For target date `t`, use only verified draws satisfying

```text
2019-05-15 <= draw_date(d) < t.
```

Let `D_t` be the number of those strictly prior draws. For every label
`i in {1,...,49}`, define

```text
c_i(t) = count of prior draws d for which i is in U_d

q_i(t) = (c_i(t) + 1) / (D_t + 7)

u_i(t) = logit(q_i(t)) - logit(1/7)
       = log((c_i(t) + 1) / (D_t - c_i(t) + 6)) - log(1/6).
```

The `Beta(1,6)` prior has the fair mean `1/7`; its posterior mean gives the
fixed add-one/add-six formula. This is a transparent fair-centred convention,
not a fitted hyperparameter. Because each historical row contributes exactly
seven union memberships,

```text
sum_i q_i(t) = (7*D_t + 49) / (D_t + 7) = 7.
```

When `D_t = 0`, every `q_i = 1/7` and every `u_i = 0`.

Map the union scores to main-number probabilities with no fitted scale:

```text
p_i(t) = logistic(alpha_t + u_i(t)),
```

where `alpha_t` is the unique deterministic intercept satisfying
`sum_i p_i(t) = 6`. When all union propensities are fair, the result is exactly
`p_i = 6/49`. A registration must freeze the stable sigmoid, intercept solver,
iteration count, probability tolerance, and ascending-label tie-break before
any V9 output exists.

The formula has no rolling window, decay, temperature, selected labels,
calendar input, sum feature, transition term, pair term, entropy gate,
change-point rule, Fourier component, role contrast, calibration fit,
constraint, or ensemble weight. It uses the complete expanding post-RNG prefix
and seed `649` only for the control described below.

## Why this is only marginally distinct

H9 has a real mechanism distinction from V7, but its statistical raw material
overlaps ordinary frequency models. Six of the seven union labels are also main
labels, so a persistent union propensity will be correlated with long-run
main-number frequency. V1 long-frequency, EMA/gap, logistic, and ensemble
models must remain fixed comparisons.

The proposed increment is limited to information those main-only counts omit:
the historical bonus is included only to estimate total seven-set selection,
while its role is discarded. This can test whether the selection-stage factor
adds stable information beyond a frozen main-only baseline. It must never be
described as an entirely new mathematical signal, and no post-hoc ensemble may
be used to manufacture separation.

The same formula is separate from the V2/V3 calendar, sum, structural, and
transition hypotheses; V5 pair affinity; V6 entropy/regime; V7 conditional
main-versus-bonus role odds; and V8 Fourier phase. It has no pair, calendar,
transition, regime, or periodic interaction.

## Targeted row-preserving negative control

A date permutation is not a useful control here because an expanding marginal
count is unchanged by reordering dates. The control must destroy persistent
label identity while preserving every draw's seven-distinct-label constraint
and role structure.

For each prior draw date `d`, construct one deterministic permutation of all
labels `1..49`:

```text
key(d, i) = SHA256(
  UTF8("lotto649-v9-union-control-v1:649:{ISO-date}:{label}")
)
```

Sort source labels by the full 32-byte digest and then ascending label, and map
the label at sorted position `j` to destination label `j+1`. Apply that one
bijective map to all six main labels and the bonus in the historical row.
Dates, row identities, seven-label uniqueness, and the main/bonus role count
remain fixed. The actual target row is never permuted and is never visible to
prediction.

Run the identical expanding counts, probability mapping, target dates, and
scoring on the relabelled strict prefixes. The date in the hash ensures each
row receives a separately fixed permutation without an RNG-library shuffle;
the mapping depends on no outcome value or future row. Literal synthetic
oracles must independently lock the hash payload, byte ordering, bijection, and
candidate/control fold identities.

The control may not enter an ensemble or support discovery. If it appears
predictive, that is a multiple-testing or pipeline warning. Candidate-minus-
control comparisons must be paired on identical prospective target dates and
resample complete target rows, never individual label cells.

## Data availability and identification limits

The committed repository data have `draw_date`, sorted main labels `n1..n6`,
and `bonus`: [`data/processed/draws.csv`](../../data/processed/draws.csv). The
immutable [`Draw`](../../src/lotto649/domain.py) object validates that the six
main labels are unique and in `1..49`, sorts them, and rejects a bonus that
duplicates a main label. These fields are sufficient to build `U_d`, compute a
strict prefix, and test future invariance.

They do not contain raw RNG bits, seed or state, software version, draw-machine
identity, scaling or shuffling calls, selection order, integrity-check result,
audit output, or certification report. Sorted `n1..n6` cannot be interpreted as
generation order. A positive association in published outcomes would be
non-identifying: it could not distinguish an RNG effect from mapping,
publication, source-processing, regime, or chance variation.

The current source review found no official draw-level equipment or software
identifier that could make a more causal historical model possible. Physical
ball weight, wear, font, or digit-shape hypotheses are inapplicable to the
post-2019 RNG regime. Modulo, residue, bit-width, and weekday hypotheses lack an
official implementation direction and would become unconstrained pattern
searches if selected from observed results.

## Prospective-only evidence protocol

The overlap audit and the exhausted V2--V8 historical record make another
historical V9 score scientifically unhelpful. Therefore the recommended lane is:

```text
all verified draws visible at freeze
  -> initialize the exact frozen expanding counts
  -> commit code, config, tests, registration, and model version
  -> create the first immutable V9 shadow snapshot before its target draw
  -> reveal and score only later eligible snapshots
  -> decide only at the pre-registered stopping point.
```

Observed history is training context, not V9 evidence. In particular,
2020--2025 is consumed diagnostic data and every 2026+ draw observed before the
freeze is consumed for this version; see
[`MODEL_PROTOCOL.md`](../MODEL_PROTOCOL.md) and
[`RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md`](../RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md). Do not calculate a V9
historical Top-K, Brier, log-loss, rank, p-value, confidence interval, year
table, or candidate-versus-control result and then decide whether to activate
it. That would make the activation decision outcome-driven.

Before the first live snapshot, offline tests may use hand-written synthetic
draws only. Required tests include:

1. exact `Beta(1,6)` count and probability oracles;
2. strict `history < target_date` and future-append invariance;
3. the 2019-05-15 boundary and complete exclusion of pre-RNG draws;
4. candidate dependence on historical seven-set membership but invariance to
   swapping historical main and bonus roles within the same seven-set;
5. invariance to the target bonus and scoring only target main labels;
6. deterministic sum-to-six probabilities in `(0,1)`;
7. exact row-permutation hash and preservation of complete row constraints;
8. rejection of duplicate, discontinuous, conflicting, or unverified data; and
9. proof that V1 production, V3 shadow, existing predictions/evaluations,
   workflows, and historical reports are unchanged.

The pre-registration must name one primary prospective metric. Mean Top-12
main-number hit lift versus exact `72/49` is compatible with the existing
protocol; per-number Brier and binary log loss versus constant `6/49` must be
mandatory proper-score gates. Complete target draws, not their six individual
labels, are the uncertainty unit. Proper probability scoring is grounded in
[Brier (1950)](https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/mwre/78/1/1520-0493_1950_078_0001_vofeit_2_0_co_2.xml)
and [Good (1952)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1952.tb00104.x); the
requirement that forecasts precede outcomes follows [Dawid's prequential
formulation](https://doi.org/10.2307/2981683).

Use the project's default minimum of 104 eligible prospective draws only if a
pre-freeze power calculation does not require more. Freeze the checkpoint,
non-overlapping halves, multiplicity family, confidence procedure, missed-or-
late-snapshot rules, negative-control gate, proper-score tolerances, and
promotion conjunction before snapshot one. No continuous peeking, optional
stopping, retrospective cohort repair, or result-inspired extension is allowed.

## Why H9 will probably fail

- The official system is independently tested, certified, integrity-checked,
  and externally witnessed. Stable number-specific final-output bias is
  contrary to its stated design and audit controls.
- The public history is final output only. It lacks the raw and production
  information that GLI requests when testing scaling, shuffling, and RNG
  behaviour, so the hypothesis is weakly mechanistic and cannot identify a
  cause.
- The candidate estimates 49 marginal propensities from a sparse seven-per-draw
  outcome. Even fixed fair-centred shrinkage can follow noise and degrade proper
  scores.
- Six sevenths of the feature are already contained in main-number frequency.
  Historical bonus membership may add no stable information beyond frozen V1
  baselines.
- Under fair sampling, some labels will look high or low over finite prefixes.
  Lottery-specific data snooping can turn whichever fluctuation is selected
  into a story; [Hassler
  (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1111/insr.12488) demonstrates this problem for a
  6/49 lottery setting.
- A positive prospective ranking fluctuation is insufficient if Brier or log
  loss worsens, fixed halves disagree, or the row-preserving control behaves
  similarly.

These failure modes are reasons to keep the model small and prospective-only,
not reasons to search historical answers for a more favourable formula.

## Pre-registration decision

The evidence supports this precise decision:

- **Deferred on 2026-08-16.** V9 was not registered, implemented, activated, or
  scored. The next registered evidence path is the unchanged V3 prospective
  cohort. Reviving H9 requires a new independent review and a prospective-only
  registration before implementation.
- **Do not perform a V9 historical model hunt.** The history available today is
  consumed for design and initialization.
- **H9 is the only candidate found that is both observable and separable at a
  documented draw-stage boundary, but it is only marginally novel.** Independent
  review may reasonably reject it before implementation because of frequency
  overlap.
- **If reviewed in, pre-register it as prospective-only `v9.0.0`.** Freeze the
  formula and control above, start from the first post-freeze committed shadow
  snapshot, and do not condition activation on a newly generated historical
  result.
- **Keep V1 production and V3 shadow.** V9 cannot replace either without the
  full documented prospective conjunction and a separate promotion PR.
- **A changed V9 begins a new cohort.** Any formula, prior, window, feature,
  mapping, control, metric, gate, or version changed after seeing a prospective
  result permanently consumes that result for the changed candidate.

If no independent reviewer accepts the marginal distinction, the scientifically
correct next action is to stop: with the available public fields, the source
review found no more independent, mechanism-backed, leakage-safe V9 family.
