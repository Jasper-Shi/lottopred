# V9 selection-stage independence review

Date: 2026-08-19

Review status: **completed; do not freeze or activate V9**. This document is an
outcome-blind primary-source and identifiability review. No V9 historical score,
backtest, prediction, p-value, confidence interval, year table, or prospective
outcome was run or inspected. No V5--V8 numerical result was used to choose the
direction below.

## Review question and decision

The candidate question is whether a post-2019 number-specific tendency to enter
the seven published LOTTO 6/49 Classic numbers -- six main numbers plus the
bonus -- is sufficiently different from both:

1. V1 main-number frequency, which counts the six main labels; and
2. V7 main-versus-bonus role bias, which contrasts the role assigned after a
   label is among the seven.

The answer is asymmetric:

| Boundary | Identifiably different? | Review finding |
|---|---:|---|
| V9 union selection versus V7 conditional role | **Yes** | The observable likelihood factors exactly, and the union statistic is invariant to any main/bonus role reassignment that preserves the seven-label set. |
| V9 union selection versus V1 main frequency | **Only marginally** | Six of seven union observations are V1 main observations. Under the fair model, their one-draw indicator correlation is about `0.915`. The only new outcome information is the one bonus label and the post-RNG boundary. |
| Worth a new frozen V9 family now | **No** | The public sources do not establish a separate production-software “select seven, then assign roles” stage, and no source fixes a label-bias mechanism or forecast scale. Testability alone does not justify a near-duplicate frequency family. |

**Decision:** keep the idea deferred. Do not add it to the experiment registry,
implement it, score it on history, or create live/shadow snapshots. The fair,
unpredictable lottery remains the default. A negative independence review is a
successful research outcome.

This decision supersedes neither the production nor shadow policy. It narrows
the earlier research basis in
[`V9_post_rng_seven_number_selection_basis.md`](V9_post_rng_seven_number_selection_basis.md):
the candidate is mathematically separable from V7, but not sufficiently
independent of V1 to merit a new frozen family on the currently available
evidence.

## Official facts and their limits

### Published game outcome

The official ILC game conditions define the Classic Draw as six main numbers
and one different bonus number, all drawn at random from `1..49`: [ILC LOTTO
6/49 Game Conditions, section
4](https://corporate.bclc.com/content/dam/bclccorporate/documents/terms-and-conditions/rules-and-regulations/lotto/lotto-649-game-conditions.pdf).
WCLC describes a bonus number for games such as LOTTO 6/49 as an additional
number drawn from the same deck using a number not already drawn: [WCLC draw
FAQ](https://www.wclc.com/faq-7.htm?channel=print).

Those sources justify treating the published seven labels as a valid observable
union. They do **not** say that the production program first creates an
unordered seven-set and then runs a separate role-assignment routine. “Selection
stage” is therefore an observable probability factorization in this review,
not a documented claim about ILC source-code architecture.

### Applicable RNG regime and fair default

Atlantic Lottery's official transition notice says that LOTTO 6/49 winning
numbers would be generated by RNG software beginning with the 2019-05-15 draw;
its integrity page says the RNG replaced mechanical ball-drop machines:
[2019 transition
notice](https://www.alc.ca/content/dam/alc/docs-en/Corp/AboutAL/Retailers/LMx2_Sellsheet_EN.PDF)
and [ALC integrity and
compliance](https://www.alc.ca/content/alc/en/corporate/about-atlantic-lottery/integrity-and-compliance.html).

Current official operational descriptions strongly favour the fair null:

- WCLC says national and regional draws use stand-alone computerized RNG draw
  machines. It says the proprietary algorithm is independently tested and
  certified, an integrity check occurs before each draw, and external auditors
  witness every draw: [WCLC Game & Draw
  Security](https://www.wclc.com/game-draw-security.htm?channel=print).
- BCLC says ILC conducts national LOTTO 6/49 draws in Toronto using RNG
  software; an independent firm audits every aspect of the draws, and
  third-party experts test and certify randomness: [BCLC, “How are Lottery
  Numbers Drawn?”](https://corporate.bclc.com/our-business/our-products/lottery/how-are-lottery-numbers-drawn-.html).
- OLG says Gaming Laboratories International reviewed and tested the actual ILC
  RNG software and certified random behaviour, with external auditors operating
  from two locations: [OLG LOTTO 6/49 RNG
  FAQ](https://www.olg.ca/en/frequently-asked-questions/lottery-games/lotto-649.html).

Accordingly, the prior direction is not “hot labels continue to be hot.” It is:
under a fair certified system, any apparent label propensity should disappear
prospectively and a frequency-based forecast should usually lose to, or fail to
improve on, constant `6/49` after proper scoring.

### RNG-to-range conversion does not identify an ILC mechanism

NIST SP 800-90A Rev. 1 Appendix A.5 specifies multiple ways to convert random
bits to bounded integers. Its simple and complex discard methods have no skew;
its simple modular method can have negligible, non-zero skew when at least 64
extra bits are used: [NIST SP 800-90A Rev. 1, Appendix
A.5](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-90Ar1).

This official conversion material establishes that bounded conversion is a
separate design choice and that multiple valid choices exist. It provides no
evidence that ILC uses any one of them, no number-label direction, and no reason
to expect a stable published bias. Selecting a modulus, residue class, bit
width, or label bucket after viewing lottery outcomes would therefore be an
unsupported search, not a mechanism test.

GLI's public testing instructions reinforce the identification limit. They ask
for production-equivalent functions, calls, variables, scaling methodology,
game range, and without-replacement rules; they distinguish final outcomes
from raw RNG output before scaling or shuffling: [GLI Technical Specifications
for RNG
Testing](https://gaminglabs.com/getting-started/technical-specifications-for-rng-testing/).
The public draw file has only final outcomes. It cannot locate a departure in
entropy generation, bounded conversion, without-replacement selection, role
assignment, or publication.

## Exact statistical decomposition

For one fixed label `i` on draw `d`, define

```text
X_id = 1 if i is one of the six main numbers, otherwise 0
Z_id = 1 if i is the bonus number, otherwise 0
U_id = X_id + Z_id = 1 if i is anywhere in the seven-number union
```

Because a label cannot be both main and bonus, write

```text
q_i = P(U_id = 1)
r_i = P(X_id = 1 | U_id = 1).
```

Then the main-number probability factors exactly:

```text
P(X_id = 1) = q_i * r_i.
```

For `D` draws, let `m_i` be the main count, `b_i` the bonus count, and
`n_i = D - m_i - b_i` the neither count. Ignoring the multinomial coefficient,
the label-specific likelihood is

```text
L(q_i, r_i)
  proportional to (q_i*r_i)^m_i
                 * (q_i*(1-r_i))^b_i
                 * (1-q_i)^n_i

  = q_i^(m_i+b_i) * (1-q_i)^n_i
    * r_i^m_i * (1-r_i)^b_i.
```

Thus `m_i+b_i` is the union-selection statistic for `q_i`, while the
main-versus-bonus split is the conditional-role statistic for `r_i`. Under the
official fair draw definition,

```text
q_i = 7/49 = 1/7
r_i = 6/7
P(X_id = 1) = 6/49.
```

Sampling seven distinct labels also means the 49 within-draw indicators are not
independent. Any uncertainty method or control must treat the complete draw as
the sampling unit. Original lottery-set work explicitly addresses uniformity
and unequal selection probabilities under sampling without replacement: [Joe
(1993)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-7152(93)90141-5) and [Johnson & Klotz
(1993)](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1993.10476320).

## Independence from V7: sufficient at the observable level

The existing V7 implementation in
[`v7_main_bonus_role_bias.py`](../../src/lotto649/models/v7_main_bonus_role_bias.py)
uses post-RNG main and bonus counts to estimate a main-to-bonus role contrast.
That is a model of `r_i`, conditional on union membership.

A union-only statistic depends on `m_i+b_i` and is unchanged if, within every
draw, the bonus role is reassigned to any of the seven observed labels while
the seven-label union is held fixed. V7's role statistic generally changes
under that operation. This gives both:

- a mathematical separation through the factorized likelihood; and
- a deterministic identity test: every within-union role permutation must
  leave a union-only forecast bit-for-bit unchanged.

The V9 idea is therefore sufficiently distinct from V7 to be identifiable from
the available fields. This conclusion does not depend on any historical model
score. It also does not prove that ILC implements two physical or software
stages; it distinguishes two observable parameters only.

## Overlap with V1: too large to justify a new family

V1's long-frequency feature is built from the main-only indicator `X_id` in
[`features.py`](../../src/lotto649/features.py), and the corresponding baseline
shrinks that main frequency toward `6/49` in
[`baselines.py`](../../src/lotto649/models/baselines.py). The proposed union
indicator is simply

```text
U_id = X_id + Z_id.
```

Under the fair model, `X_id U_id = X_id`. Therefore

```text
Corr(X_id, U_id)^2
  = [P(X=1) * (1-P(U=1))]
    / [P(U=1) * (1-P(X=1))]

  = [(6/49)*(42/49)] / [(7/49)*(43/49)]
  = 252/301,

Corr(X_id, U_id) = sqrt(252/301) ~= 0.914991.
```

For independent fair draws, the same correlation holds for their accumulated
counts. Six sevenths of positive union memberships are already positive main
memberships. The only additional label observation per draw is the bonus.

The post-2019 start boundary is operationally justified, but it does not create
a new feature mechanism: it is a regime restriction on another frequency
estimator. A union candidate could be called an incremental bonus-augmented
frequency probe. It could not honestly be called an independent new signal
family.

This high overlap is not a formal collinearity impossibility: bonus labels can
make `m_i+b_i` differ from `m_i`. It is a scientific-value judgment. With no
officially documented separate selection routine and no production metadata,
one extra label per draw is too little mechanism-specific information to
justify another registered model family.

## Data boundary

The committed processed data expose `draw_date`, six sorted main labels
`n1..n6`, and `bonus`:
[`data/processed/draws.csv`](../../data/processed/draws.csv). The repository's
immutable [`Draw`](../../src/lotto649/domain.py) validates label range,
main-number uniqueness, and bonus separation from the main set.

Those fields are sufficient for the observable decomposition and for a strict
prefix calculation. They do not expose:

- RNG bits, seed, state, entropy-source diagnostics, or raw pre-scaling output;
- bounded-conversion, shuffle, or without-replacement implementation details;
- software version, draw-machine identifier, or deployment/change history;
- generation order -- sorted `n1..n6` is not draw order;
- integrity-check records, auditor output, or the private certification report.

Any candidate revived in the future would have to use only verified draws with
`2019-05-15 <= draw_date < target_date`, require a non-missing bonus for every
included row, and predict only the six main labels. The target draw and target
bonus must never enter features. All outcomes visible before a freeze are
training context or consumed diagnostics, not confirmatory evidence.

## Falsifiable prospective skeleton -- not an active registration

The following is included to show that the idea is strictly testable. It is
**not** approval to implement, register, backtest, or activate it.

For target draw `t`, let `D_t` be the number of verified post-RNG draws strictly
before `t`, and let

```text
c_i(t) = sum over prior d of U_id.
```

A minimal fair-centred, no-fit convention would be

```text
q_hat_i(t) = (c_i(t) + 1) / (D_t + 7)
p_hat_i(t) = (6/7) * q_hat_i(t).
```

Because each prior row contributes exactly seven union memberships,

```text
sum_i q_hat_i(t) = 7
sum_i p_hat_i(t) = 6.
```

The directional claim would be: a label with a higher strictly prior union
count receives a higher next-draw main probability, while the role factor is
fixed at fair `6/7`. It is falsified prospectively if this exact forecast fails
to improve the pre-registered primary score over constant `6/49`, fails to add
value beyond the paired bonus-destroyed control below, or worsens mandatory
proper scores.

The `+1` and `+7` are a transparent `Beta(1,6)`-marginal smoothing convention,
not an official property of the RNG. A continuum
`(c_i+a)/(D_t+7a)` is possible. That unresolved scientific arbitrariness is an
additional reason not to freeze the family now. Historical performance must
never be used to choose `a`.

If genuinely new, outcome-independent mechanism evidence later reopens the
question, the only acceptable bounded version from this review would freeze all
of the following before snapshot one:

| Item | Frozen value |
|---|---|
| Regime boundary | `2019-05-15`, inclusive |
| History | Complete expanding verified prefix; no window or decay |
| Labels | All integers `1..49`; no selected subset or bucket |
| Observable | Seven-number union only; historical roles collapsed |
| Smoothing | `a=1`; no search or fitting |
| Main mapping | Multiply by exactly `6/7`; no sigmoid, scale, calibration, or fitted intercept |
| Output contract | 49 probabilities in label order, expected total exactly six |
| Target | Six main labels only; target bonus prohibited |
| Controls | Exact targeted control below plus constant `6/49` and frozen V1 comparison |
| Randomness | No candidate randomness; control domain and seed `649` fixed |
| Activation | No burn-in selected from outcomes; evidence starts at the first immutable post-freeze snapshot |

No window, threshold, coefficient, temperature, label group, calendar input,
pair term, entropy gate, role term, spectral term, calibration fit, ensemble
weight, or constraint may be added. Any change after an outcome is visible
creates a new version and a new prospective cohort.

## Targeted negative control for the V1 overlap

The earlier full-row label-permutation control destroys V1 main-label identity
as well as bonus identity. It is useful as a generic null control but cannot
isolate whether a union candidate adds anything beyond V1 main frequency.

The independence review requires a more targeted paired control. For every
strictly prior historical draw `d`:

1. preserve its date and six main labels exactly;
2. ignore the observed bonus;
3. among the 43 labels outside the main set, choose the deterministic synthetic
   bonus with the smallest full SHA-256 digest of

```text
UTF8(
  "lotto649-v9-bonus-increment-control-v1:649:{ISO-date}:{candidate-label}"
)
```

with ascending candidate label as the digest-tie fallback; and

4. run the identical union-count formula on the six real main labels plus that
   synthetic bonus.

This construction leaves every V1 main-frequency input unchanged, preserves
the six-main-plus-one-distinct-bonus row constraint, uses no future or target
field, and destroys only the observed bonus-label contribution that purports to
make V9 incremental. Candidate and control must be evaluated on the same
prospective target draws with complete draws as the resampling unit.

Interpretation is fixed in advance:

- if candidate and control behave alike, there is no evidence of value beyond
  the V1-overlapping main-frequency component;
- if the control appears predictive, treat that as a pipeline or multiplicity
  warning, not a discovery; and
- a candidate advantage over this control is necessary but not sufficient --
  it must also beat the fair baseline without degrading proper scores.

The within-union role-swap operation from the V7 section is an identity test,
not another selectable performance control. Keeping one targeted randomized
control avoids creating a new control-selection garden of forking paths.

## Why the hypothesis is likely to fail

- The official RNG is proprietary but independently tested, certified,
  integrity-checked before each draw, and externally witnessed. Stable final
  label bias is contrary to the stated design and controls.
- Public outcomes are downstream of every hidden implementation step. A
  positive association could not identify an RNG, conversion, selection,
  role-assignment, source-processing, or publication cause.
- The purported increment beyond V1 is only one bonus label per draw and is
  highly correlated with main-only frequency under the null.
- Forty-nine finite-prefix label counts will fluctuate even when all labels are
  exchangeable. Choosing a direction, window, scale, or label subset after
  seeing those fluctuations would manufacture a story. A peer-reviewed 6/49
  case study shows how lottery-pattern snooping can exaggerate an apparent
  directional effect: [Hassler
  (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1111/insr.12488).
- Unknown production-version changes could break persistence even if a genuine
  final-output association once existed, and the public file has no version
  field with which to define such a regime.

## Final disposition

1. **Distinct from V7:** yes, as an observable `q_i` parameter, proven by the
   factorized likelihood and role-swap invariance.
2. **Distinct from V1:** only marginally, not enough for a new model family;
   the fair-null indicator correlation is approximately `0.915` and the only
   new outcome is the bonus label.
3. **Mechanism support:** insufficient. Official sources establish the output,
   RNG regime, audit controls, and conversion possibilities, but not a separate
   ILC selection stage or a directional label mechanism.
4. **Pre-registration decision:** **do not freeze V9**. Leave it deferred and
   do not create an experiment-registry entry, implementation, historical
   report, or snapshot.
5. **Reopening rule:** only new outcome-independent external evidence -- for
   example official implementation/audit documentation or draw-level raw and
   version metadata -- may justify another review. Historical or prospective
   lottery outcomes must not be used to reverse this decision and then be
   presented as confirmation for the changed candidate.

Stopping here is preferable to inventing a less defensible feature family from
already observed outcomes.
