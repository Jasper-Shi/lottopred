# V11 primary-source basis: previous-bonus carryover

Date: 2026-08-19

Research status: primary-source, mathematics, and overlap review only. **No V11
historical prediction, backtest, score, target outcome, p-value, confidence
interval, or model output was generated or inspected.** Existing V1--V10
results were read only to prevent duplicate hypotheses and to preserve the
multiple-testing record; they did not select the sign, formula, boundary, or
control below.

Proposed slug: `v11_previous_bonus_carryover`

## Decision

The one outcome-blind hypothesis reviewed here is:

> **H11:** In the current RNG regime, the published bonus label from the
> immediately preceding LOTTO 6/49 Classic Draw has a stable signed residual
> tendency to enter the six main labels of the next draw, after accounting for
> the exact frozen V1 ensemble probability for that label.

**Decision: FREEZE-ELIGIBLE only as one narrow, research-only, falsification-
first V11 registration.** The sole formula is a direct marginal logit offset
for the previous-bonus anchor plus a deterministic capacity-proportional
transfer over the other 48 frozen V1 marginals. The candidate has one learned
scalar, one externally fixed regime boundary, one official role input that
V1/V3 do not use, and a targeted within-seven-set role control. It is
sufficiently different from V7 and the deferred V9 observable to be
identifiable. It is nevertheless a variant of the already exposed
transition/Markov family, not a new independent family, and its multiplicity
count may not restart.

The expected result is negative. Official sources describe independently
tested, certified RNG software and controls intended to produce random,
independent outcomes. No official source says that the bonus is the last RNG
call, that RNG state carries from one draw to the next, or that a previous bonus
has any privileged next-draw status. The hypothesis is therefore a bounded
final-output serial-dependence audit, not a claim about ILC source code.

This note is not an experiment registration and authorizes no scoring. A later
registration would have to freeze the exact numerical solver, baseline commit,
data fingerprint, target lane, multiplicity family, controls, metrics, and
decision conjunction before any V11 output exists. Any 2020--2025 result would
be consumed historical diagnostic evidence only. Model confirmation could
begin only with immutable post-freeze predictions.

## Why this observable is legitimate but the mechanism prior is weak

### Official facts

1. The current ILC game conditions define a Classic Draw as six main numbers
   and one different bonus number, all drawn at random from `1..49`. They define
   `6/6` using only the six main numbers. Thus `main` and `bonus` are official
   published roles, and only the next draw's six main labels may be prediction
   targets. See the [ILC LOTTO 6/49 Game
   Conditions](https://corporate.bclc.com/content/dam/bclccorporate/documents/terms-and-conditions/rules-and-regulations/lotto/lotto-649-game-conditions.pdf),
   sections 4, 5, and 10.

2. WCLC explains the game-level role using a same-deck analogy: for games such
   as LOTTO 6/49, the bonus is an additional number from the same deck, using a
   label not already drawn. This supports conditioning on the published bonus
   role; it does **not** disclose software call order. See the [WCLC draw
   FAQ](https://www.wclc.com/faq-7.htm?channel=print), under the question about
   Grand Number versus bonus number.

3. The current national draws use RNG software on stand-alone computerized draw
   machines. WCLC says the proprietary algorithm is independently tested and
   certified, an integrity check occurs before every draw, and external auditors
   witness the draws. BCLC says LOTTO 6/49 national draws are conducted in
   Toronto and that third-party experts test and certify the RNG software. See
   [WCLC Game & Draw Security](https://www.wclc.com/game-draw-security.htm)
   and [BCLC, How are Lottery Numbers
   Drawn?](https://corporate.bclc.com/our-business/our-products/lottery/how-are-lottery-numbers-drawn-.html).

4. OLG states that ILC moved LOTTO 6/49 and LOTTO MAX to RNG draws in May 2019.
   It also states that GLI reviewed the RNG software used for LOTTO 6/49, LOTTO
   MAX, and DAILY GRAND and certified random behaviour. A contemporaneous
   Atlantic Lottery retailer notice identifies the first LOTTO 6/49 winning
   numbers generated by RNG software as those for May 15, 2019. This fixes the
   exact `2019-05-15` mechanism boundary independently of lottery outcomes.
   See the official [OLG RNG
   FAQ](https://www.olg.ca/en/frequently-asked-questions/lottery-games/lotto-max.html),
   under the RNG questions, and [Atlantic Lottery's 2019 transition
   notice](https://www.alc.ca/content/dam/alc/docs-en/Corp/AboutAL/Retailers/LMx2_Sellsheet_EN.PDF),
   page 2.

5. GLI-11 v3.0 is a public general standard, not proof of the private ILC
   engagement terms. It nevertheless states the relevant intended null: source
   review covers RNG, scaling, shuffling, and mapping; final output is tested for
   its intended distribution and independence between draws; possible tests
   include overlaps and serial correlation; and each possible RNG selection is
   to be equally likely. See [GLI-11 v3.0, sections
   3.2.1--3.2.5](https://gaminglabs.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/GLI-11-Gaming-Devices-V3-0.pdf).

6. GLI's public submission instructions ask for production-equivalent functions,
   calls, variables, scaling, range, and without-replacement rules, and
   distinguish raw RNG output from final output. Public winning rows have none
   of that internal evidence. See [GLI Technical Specifications for RNG
   Testing](https://gaminglabs.com/getting-started/technical-specifications-for-rng-testing/).

### What H11 can and cannot mean

For consecutive draws `d-1` and `d`, let `B_(d-1)` be the prior published bonus
label and `M_d` the next six-main set. Under independent fair draws,

```text
P(B_(d-1) in M_d | every value available before draw d) = 6/49.
```

This follows because, conditional on any prior bonus label, six of 49 labels
occupy the next main roles. Within one target draw the 49 inclusion indicators
are dependent because six labels are sampled without replacement; across draws
the official intended model is independence.

A stable departure could be consistent with final-output serial dependence,
state reuse, mapping/shuffling dependence, a publication transformation, or a
data-pipeline defect. Published rows cannot distinguish those causes. George
Marsaglia's original paper shows that some congruential pseudorandom generators
can have multivariate lattice structure ([Marsaglia
1968](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.61.1.25)), but that paper is **not** evidence
that ILC uses such a generator or that its certified system has this defect.

The term `bonus-to-next-main` refers only to official published roles. H11 must
not be described as “the seventh RNG output influences the eighth,” because no
reviewed official source exposes generation order or state continuity.

## The one fixed candidate formula

The formula below is the sole freeze-eligible candidate from this review. The
alternatives listed later are excluded, not a grid.

### Strict-prefix transition rows

Let `t` be a target date. Let the verified post-RNG draws strictly before `t`
be

```text
D_0, D_1, ..., D_(n-1),
```

in increasing date order, with `draw_date(D_0) >= 2019-05-15`. For every
transition whose source and destination are both in that sequence, define

```text
a_j = bonus label of D_(j-1)
y_j = 1[a_j is in the six main labels of D_j],       j = 1,...,n-1.
```

For destination draw `D_j`, reconstruct the exact frozen V1 ensemble forecast
using **all verified draws before `D_j` and no draw on or after `D_j`**. Denote
its 49 marginal probabilities by `b_(j,i)` and set

```text
q_b,j = b_(j,a_j).
```

The V1 reconstruction is part of the feature. It must be frozen to one exact
commit/config/runtime and must pass the same chronology and probability
contract as a live snapshot. A cached value keyed by only history length or
tail date is not acceptable evidence; the cache key must bind the complete
visible history.

### Direct V1 residual estimand

V1 returns exactly the object V11 needs: 49 inclusion marginals in `(0,1)`.
Their real-number expected-count target is six; the frozen binary64 contract is
`abs(math.fsum(b.values()) - 6.0) <= 1e-12`, not bitwise equality to `6.0`.
V1 does **not** specify a joint probability law for the complete six-number
set. V11 must preserve that boundary rather than invent a joint completion.
Its only fitted estimand is the common signed log-odds residual for the event
that the previous bonus anchor enters the next main set.

### One normally regularized scalar offset

Write

```text
p0 = 6/49
sigmoid(z) = 1 / (1 + exp(-z)), evaluated stably.
```

Estimate exactly one scalar `beta_t` from the visible transition prefix. For
each historical anchor event, define

```text
r_j(beta) = sigmoid(logit(q_b,j) + beta).
```

Use the standard-normal prior `beta ~ N(0,1)`. The expanding MAP estimate
uniquely maximizes, up to constants independent of `beta`,

```text
ell_t(beta) =
    - beta^2 / 2
  + sum[j=1..n-1] {
        y_j * beta - log(1 - q_b,j + q_b,j * exp(beta))
    }.
```

Equivalently, `beta_t` is the unique finite root of

```text
U_t(beta) = -beta + sum[j=1..n-1] (y_j - r_j(beta)) = 0.
```

The score is continuous and strictly decreasing because

```text
U'_t(beta) = -1 - sum[j] r_j(beta) * (1 - r_j(beta)) < 0.
```

It tends to positive infinity as `beta` tends to negative infinity and to
negative infinity as `beta` tends to positive infinity. The root therefore
exists and is unique even when every observed recurrence is zero or one. With
no eligible transition it is exactly `beta_t = 0`.

The normal scale is exactly `1`. It is a scoring-before-results regularization
choice, not an estimated uncertainty or a candidate hyperparameter. The scale,
prior family, and MAP convention may not be tuned, replaced, or compared after
any V11 output is visible.

The registration must freeze stable logit/sigmoid evaluation, IEEE-754
arithmetic, summation order, root bracket, iteration count, equality branch,
and exact synthetic oracles. Bracket expansion, early exit, coefficient
clipping, and result-driven solver changes are prohibited.

### Target marginals and capacity-proportional transfer

For target `t`, compute the exact frozen V1 ensemble vector `b_(t,i)` from the
complete strict history and define

```text
a_t       = bonus label of the latest verified draw before t
q_b,t     = b_(t,a_t)
q_t       = sigmoid(logit(q_b,t) + beta_t)
delta_t   = q_t - q_b,t.
```

The normative equality branch is: if `beta_t = 0`, set `q_t = q_b,t` and
`delta_t = 0` directly, without evaluating `logit` or `sigmoid`.

The anchor marginal is `p_(t,a_t) = q_t`. The sole normalization for every
non-anchor label is the following capacity-proportional transfer:

```text
if delta_t > 0:       # anchor probability rises
    p_(t,i) = b_(t,i) - delta_t * b_(t,i) / (6 - q_b,t)
            = b_(t,i) * (6 - q_t) / (6 - q_b,t)

if delta_t < 0:       # anchor probability falls
    p_(t,i) = b_(t,i)
               + (-delta_t) * (1 - b_(t,i)) / (42 + q_b,t)

if delta_t = 0:
    p_(t,i) = b_(t,i),

for every i != a_t.
```

The two denominators are not tunable. Before transfer, the other 48 labels
have total downward capacity

```text
sum[i != a_t] b_(t,i) = 6 - q_b,t
```

and total upward capacity

```text
sum[i != a_t] (1 - b_(t,i)) = 42 + q_b,t.
```

These capacity identities and conservation at exactly six hold in ideal real
arithmetic under the mathematical V1 contract. Every ideal output remains
strictly in `(0,1)`. The binary64 implementation must apply the formulas as
written and require

```text
abs(math.fsum(p.values()) - 6.0) <= 1e-12.
```

It must fail rather than repair, rescale, or renormalize a vector outside that
tolerance. In the rising branch every non-anchor probability receives the same
positive multiplicative factor. In the falling branch each becomes the same
positive affine transformation of its V1 value. The non-anchor ordering is
therefore unchanged in both branches. With the frozen ranking tie rule, the
Top-6 final combination can differ from V1 by at most the anchor entering or
leaving, hence by at most one swapped label.

When `beta_t = 0`, the implementation must return the original 49 V1 values
and ranking **bit for bit**, without a logit round-trip, transfer, or
renormalization. This is the exact V1 ablation. Stable evaluation is still
required for nonzero `beta_t`, but no result-dependent clipping or repair is
permitted.

### Prefix-predictable anchor evidence

After the already-frozen prediction for target `t` is revealed, let

```text
y_t = 1[a_t is in the target six-main set].
```

The fair-null one-step e-factor is

```text
e_t = (q_t / p0)^y_t
      * ((1 - q_t) / (1 - p0))^(1 - y_t),    p0 = 6/49.
```

Because `q_t` is fixed from the strict prefix and the fair conditional null has
`P(y_t=1 | prefix)=p0`, `E[e_t | prefix]=1`. Thus the cumulative product of
the nonnegative `e_t` factors is the anchor-event fair e-process. Its threshold,
multiplicity treatment, and any stopping rule must be frozen before outcomes;
the e-process is not a complete-six-set probability model.

For stable reporting and gating, define its log exactly as

```text
log_g_t = y_t * log(q_t / p0)
        + (1 - y_t) * log((1 - q_t) / (1 - p0)).
```

The one-step log gain relative to the actual V1 anchor marginal is

```text
d_t = y_t * log(q_t / q_b,t)
      + (1 - y_t) * log((1 - q_t) / (1 - q_b,t)).
```

The cumulative `sum_t d_t` measures only whether the registered anchor offset
improves the V1 anchor-event forecast. At `beta_t = 0`, `e_t` reduces to the V1-
anchor-versus-fair factor and `d_t = 0` exactly.

V11 intentionally specifies no complete six-set joint distribution, no exact-
combination probability, and no complete-set logarithmic score. The marginal
vector is sufficient for the repository's probability contract, Top-K ranking,
and expected-hit-optimal Top-6 final combination. Completing V1's marginals by
a 48-dimensional maximum-entropy inverse would add an unaudited joint modeling
assumption without adding information about `beta`; it is explicitly excluded.

Required output remains the complete marginal probability vector and ranking
for labels `1..49`, Top-6, Top-12, Top-18, and the marginal Top-6 final
combination. Every probability must be finite and in `(0,1)`, and their
binary64 total must satisfy `abs(math.fsum(p.values()) - 6.0) <= 1e-12`
without repair. No hard role, sum, gap, adjacency, or combination constraint is
permitted.

## Targeted negative controls

### Fixed predictive role control

For each post-RNG source draw `D_j`, hold its date and seven-label union

```text
U_j = main(D_j) union {bonus(D_j)}
```

fixed, but select a deterministic pseudo-bonus from the seven labels by the
minimum full SHA-256 digest of

```text
UTF8("lotto649-v11-bonus-anchor-control-v1:649:{ISO-date}:{label}")
```

with ascending label as the impossible digest-tie fallback. Use that pseudo-
bonus only as the source anchor for the H11 recurrence feature. Keep the actual
main-only history, V1 ensemble, next-main target, dates, and seven-label unions
unchanged. Run the identical scalar estimator, direct anchor offset,
capacity-proportional transfer, `e_t`, `log_g_t`, and `d_t`, replacing only the source
anchor role.

The frozen literal oracle is: for date `2020-01-01` and source union
`{1,2,3,4,5,6,7}`, the selected pseudo-bonus is label `4`, whose full digest is

```text
1052da1f3ebf9c1bfe2f06998f13ebc812c01dd08fd9b0b21cc20fd35d0840c8
```

under the exact UTF-8 payload above. An implementation must reproduce this
label and digest before any evidence run.

This control deliberately may nominate one of the six actual prior-main labels.
The V1 offset `b_(j,a_j)` already accounts for prior-main membership and other
frozen baseline features. If this pseudo-role control also appears predictive,
the candidate has not isolated a bonus-specific residual and activation must be
blocked. The seed/hash mapping may never be retried.

### Deferred global conditional role audit

A separate Monte Carlo audit that reassigns the source bonus role within each
observed seven-set could test a broader role-specific serial association, but
it is **not part of V11**. Its statistic, randomization count, tie rule, and
inferential role are not uniquely justified by the mechanism evidence above.
Adding it now would create another selectable analysis after the hypothesis was
chosen. V11 therefore implements and scores only the deterministic SHA-256
pseudo-bonus predictive control specified above. No global role-randomization
statistic, p-value, gate, or rescue analysis may be computed or reported under
this registration. Any future global audit requires a new outcome-blind
registration and cannot alter the V11 decision.

Whole-date permutations, independent label-cell shuffles, alternate control
seeds, and whichever of several audit statistics gives the smallest p-value are
likewise excluded.

### Project-wide fair-random benchmark

The project protocol requires the existing target-date-seeded `random v1.0.0`
model in every benchmark. It is therefore retained on the same target dates as
a descriptive fair sanity control and as a separately frozen Final-6
opportunity. Its seed is `649000000 + target_date.toordinal()` and its
implementation is part of the frozen V1 dependency closure. It never enters
the beta estimate, the transition-family Holm calculation, or a discovery
contrast, and it can never support H11. A non-null random result is an audit
warning. This benchmark is distinct from the deferred global role audit.

## Strict data and chronology boundary

For every target `t`:

1. Require a unique, continuous, verified draw chronology and a non-missing,
   non-conflicting bonus for every eligible post-RNG row.
2. Use only transitions whose source and destination dates are both before
   `t`. The latest training response is therefore the latest **already
   revealed** main set; no transition ending at `t` enters `beta_t`.
3. The target anchor is only the bonus from the latest draw before `t`. The
   target's main set, bonus, source payload, and every later draw are prohibited
   until after the probability/ranking snapshot is durably frozen.
4. Build every historical V1 base forecast from the history strictly before its
   own destination draw. A V1 forecast reconstructed using the final prefix,
   full-block normalization, or future-fitted preprocessing is leakage.
5. Use every eligible post-2019 transition; no rolling window, decay, weekday,
   month, hand-picked start, skipped loss, or outcome-dependent burn-in is
   allowed. Pre-2019 ball-machine transitions do not enter `beta_t`.
6. Hash and commit the exact input prefix. Existing `predictions/` and
   `evaluations/` are immutable and may not be regenerated for V11.

The repository currently exposes exactly `draw_date`, sorted main labels
`n1..n6`, and `bonus` in
[`data/processed/draws.csv`](../../data/processed/draws.csv); the immutable
[`Draw`](../../src/lotto649/domain.py) validates the six-set and bonus
separation. These fields are sufficient for H11. They do not expose draw order,
RNG bits/state, software version, machine identifier, scaling calls, audit
records, or certification test output, so causal claims about an implementation
remain impossible.

Historical evidence lanes remain unchanged:

- `1982--2014` and `2015--2019` do not test the registered post-RNG mechanism;
- `2020--2025` is a consumed diagnostic lane and can never confirm V11;
- every 2026+ outcome visible before the V11 freeze is training context or
  consumed evidence for this version; and
- confirmation starts only with the exact frozen version's first immutable
  post-freeze prediction.

If a one-shot consumed historical diagnostic is registered, each target must
follow `predict/freeze -> reveal -> score` in a durable hash-chained ledger.
The diagnostic can reject V11 or justify a separate reviewed prospective-shadow
decision; it cannot prove a predictive edge.

## Evaluation requirements if V11 is registered

Because H11 is a residual on top of V1, beating fair theory alone is not an
incremental result. A registration should freeze one primary ranking estimand
and require all of the following as a conjunction:

1. a paired candidate-minus-frozen-V1-ensemble Top-12 improvement, with the
   complete target draw as the uncertainty unit;
2. positive candidate Top-12 lift over exact fair `72/49`, with the append-only
   transition-family multiplicity correction;
3. no degradation in 49-label Brier score or binary log loss versus both the
   frozen V1 ensemble and constant fair `6/49` in the aggregate and fixed
   non-overlapping halves;
4. positive cumulative relative-V1 anchor log gain `sum_t d_t` in the aggregate
   and both fixed halves, plus the fair-null anchor e-process `product_t e_t`
   crossing its multiplicity-adjusted, pre-registered evidence threshold;
5. candidate-minus-fixed-control improvement, with both the deterministic
   fixed control and project-wide fair-random benchmark behaving as null; the
   deferred global conditional role audit is not computed, gated, or available
   as a rescue analysis in V11;
6. exact chronology, source, Git, dependency, probability-contract, and
   immutable-snapshot audits with no unresolved warning.

Also report Top-6/12/18 hits and fair lifts, mean actual rank, calibration,
year/fixed-era results, Brier/log loss, final-six hit histogram `0..6`, and every
3/6-or-higher date. A chance 6/6 must trigger the project's full leakage and
selection audit; it cannot override a violated frozen protocol.

Prequential evaluation follows Dawid's requirement that forecasts precede
observations ([Dawid 1984](https://doi.org/10.2307/2981683)). Probability
scores should use the original Brier and logarithmic-score foundations
([Brier 1950](https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/mwre/78/1/1520-0493_1950_078_0001_vofeit_2_0_co_2.xml);
[Good 1952](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1952.tb00104.x)). Lottery
sampling is without replacement, so draw-level inference must respect the
complete set rather than treating 49 cells as independent; see [Johnson and
Klotz (1993)](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1993.10476320) and [Joe
(1993)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-7152(93)90141-5).

## Excluded variants

Freezing this note does not permit a search over related formulas. The following
are different hypotheses/versions and are excluded from V11:

- a 49-by-49 bonus-to-number transition matrix;
- per-bonus-label, per-weekday, Wednesday/Saturday, month, jackpot, or era
  coefficients;
- lags other than exactly one draw, multiple lags, or “seen in last `k`” bonus
  features;
- main-plus-bonus union frequency, main-versus-bonus role odds, or a V7/V9
  ensemble;
- rolling windows, EMA/decay, change points, selected burn-ins, temperature,
  clipping, coefficient grid, an alternate prior family, or a normal prior
  scale other than exactly `1`;
- an anchor-only fair-`6/49` baseline, a compensating logit intercept over the
  other 48 V1 marginals, or any transfer other than the two fixed capacity-
  proportional branches above;
- maximum-entropy, rejective, independent-Bernoulli, or any other completion of
  the V1 marginals into a joint distribution or complete-set score;
- adding H11 to V3, retraining V1 weights, or choosing a base model after V11
  scores are seen;
- a sign constrained after observing whether bonus recurrence is high or low;
- target bonus use, generation-order claims, or hard inclusion/exclusion of the
  prior bonus in the final six;
- alternate controls, control seeds, primary metrics, halves, or stopping rules
  selected after any score is visible.

The only candidate is the normally regularized signed scalar anchor offset plus
the fixed capacity-proportional marginal transfer above on the exact frozen V1
ensemble, with a complete expanding post-RNG transition prefix.

## Why V11 will probably fail

- The current draw system is explicitly designed, independently tested, and
  audited for random output and independence. A stable cross-draw bonus-role
  residual contradicts the stated null.
- No public implementation record supports bonus-specific state carryover. The
  official bonus role is a semantic boundary, not proof of software call order.
- The model obtains only one binary recurrence observation per draw and tilts
  only one anchor-inclusion statistic. Its induced marginal changes, statistical
  power, and possible Top-K gain are inherently limited.
- The frozen V1 base already models main-label frequency, recent membership,
  and gaps. Apparent bonus recurrence can be ordinary label-frequency noise or
  baseline miscalibration rather than a role-specific transition.
- A within-seven-set pseudo-role control can show a similar fluctuation. If it
  does, the mechanism is not isolated.
- The hypothesis was proposed after many feature families had already been
  exposed. Honest append-only multiplicity correction makes a chance nominal
  result insufficient.
- Statistical tests cannot certify unpredictability or identify a proprietary
  RNG cause. NIST explicitly warns that a test suite is only one part of RNG
  assessment and cannot replace design analysis or cryptanalysis; see [NIST SP
  800-22 Rev. 1a](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-22r1a).

These are reasons to expect and accept rejection, not reasons to tune another
lag, window, role, or coefficient after seeing results.

## Freeze verdict

H11 clears the narrow source-review threshold that V9 did not:

- its new input is an official published role absent from V1/V3;
- its estimand is temporal and label-agnostic, rather than another highly
  correlated marginal-frequency count;
- it adds exactly one signed scalar to an unchanged marginal baseline, preserves
  every non-anchor ordering, and returns V1 bit for bit when that scalar is
  zero;
- the fair null is exact, the prefix boundary is explicit, and the source-role
  control targets the claimed increment; and
- failure closes the exact direction without opening a parameter search.

Accordingly, it is worth **freezing**, but not yet worth believing. The correct
next artifact would be a separate, reviewable preregistration that copies the
one direct-marginal formula verbatim, assigns it to the existing
transition/Markov
multiplicity ledger, and prohibits V11 scoring until the registration commit
is immutable. V1 remains production, V3 remains shadow, and no V11 live role
follows from this basis note.
